Rice blast disease affects leaves and reduces yield. Use resistant varieties and fungicides.

Tomato requires well-drained soil and moderate watering. Avoid overwatering.

Aphids are pests that damage crops. Use neem oil spray or natural predators.

Soil fertility improves with compost and organic manure.

Nitrogen helps leafy growth but excess causes damage.

Wheat grows in cool climate with moderate rainfall.

Maize needs phosphorus-rich soil for better yield.

Cotton is affected by bollworm pests.

Drip irrigation saves water and improves efficiency.

Banana plants need potassium-rich soil for good fruit.
